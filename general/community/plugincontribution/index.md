Moodle Marketplace is the current place to publish and discover Moodle plugins. To submit a plugin, follow the [Moodle Marketplace Plugin Submission Guidelines](https://moodle.atlassian.net/wiki/external/ODRhYjAyNTY4NDVmNGJlNjljN2ViMzkwYzdmYmIwMGI).

For general guidance on developing Moodle plugins, see [Plugin types](/docs/apis/plugintypes/), [Frankenstyle](../../development/policies/codingstyle/frankenstyle.md), and [Plugin documentation](../../community/plugincontribution/documentation.md).

If you are contributing to Moodle core rather than publishing a plugin, see the [Moodle developer community overview](../../community/intro.md).
